4Group detail

Split by night, the same as the room numbers. Blocked is the rooms that group holds that night. Picked up means the activity report shows nothing left to pick up, so the actual guest count is used and occupancy is locked; otherwise sleepers = occupancy × blocked rooms. Default occ is averaged across the night’s room types — type over it and it applies to every night of that group, and any extra sleepers are added on to the Ten Day On Books figures. Dining arrangements are filled in from the event schedule and the res diary, and can be edited per night. Category shows where this group dines: a dining line naming La Cucina or Tre-Dodridge is the restaurant, one naming Vale Grill the grill, and anything else a Function. Only a Function adds the group’s sleepers to the Diners section; the Vale Grill and La Cucina | Tre-Dodridge lines are counted from res diary covers instead. A res diary line in some other area cannot make the group a Function. Where the dining names more than one place the restaurant wins. Put notes in Extra comments, which is free text and never moves a figure. Bookings named “allocation” are excluded.

5Diners

Sleepers is everyone staying across both sites, from Ten Day On Books, including the guests in rooms not yet picked up. Vale Grill (residents) and La Cucina | Tre-Dodridge (residents) are the total res diary covers booked that night in each area (Vale Grill includes the conservatory); lunches and afternoon teas are left out. The non-residents lines start at 0 and nothing is typed in: use → non-res under a resident figure to move one cover down to the non-resident line (+1 non-resident, −1 resident), and ← back to undo one. Functions (residents) is the total sleepers of the groups whose dining names somewhere other than those outlets. Possible Diners = sleepers − residents only; non-resident figures are never subtracted, as those people were never sleepers.

Updates as soon as you edit a dining arrangement — use this to force a re-check.

How the figures are calculated
  • Rooms Sold, Adults and Children = Group IH Tonight + Transient IH Tonight + Rooms Not P/U Tonight, using the rms, adult and child columns.
  • Not P/U is a room held but not yet picked up. Those rooms count as sold and the people in them count as sleepers.
  • Rooms in house = Group + Transient, so rooms sold less the Not P/U figure.
  • Total Sleepers = Adults + Children.
  • Rooms Available = total rooms − rooms sold (Vale 143, Hensol 23 by default). This matches Vacant Rooms Tonight on the source report.
  • Occupancy % = rooms sold ÷ total rooms — the same basis as Occupancy Tonight on the source report.
  • Diners — each outlet has a residents line and a non-residents line. Vale Grill (residents) and La Cucina | Tre-Dodridge (residents) are the res diary covers booked in that area that night. A non-residents line is 0 unless covers have been moved down from the residents line above it. Functions (residents) is the sleepers of every group whose dining names somewhere other than those outlets. Only the resident lines come off the sleeper count.
  • Day 1 = the date in the file name plus 4 days, unless overridden above.
